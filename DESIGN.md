# Sea-Glass Civic System

## Direction

The MDRRMO portal is a contemporary municipal service for residents and response officers. Its visual language pairs a calm, ocean-toned service shell with clear emergency states and familiar web controls. Preserve the Kalibo emblem and identify the operating group as Kalibo MDRRMO Special Operations Unit (SOU).

## Palette

- Canvas: mist blue-green `#eaf2f4`; night water `#0e1c24`.
- Main surface: cool white `#f8fbfc`; dark surface `#142630`.
- Ink: deep teal `#102a34`; dark-mode ink `#f2f7f8`.
- Service blue: `#155f73`, deep `#10384e`, with cobalt in the masthead gradient.
- Sea-glass support: `#e6f1f0` and `#57b2c1`.
- Amber is reserved for public warnings and primary action; coral/red is reserved for critical conditions and errors.
- Focus: high-contrast cyan-teal in light mode, pale aqua in dark mode.

## Materials and components

- Use gradients to distinguish the service masthead and broad page canvases, not as text effects or decorative blobs.
- Use translucent glass sparingly at the shared masthead, summary panels, and useful overlays; keep forms and dense work areas readable and stable.
- Use soft inset depth for inputs and quiet panels. Keep one crisp, dark-edged accent for the urgent hotline and primary action rather than repeating hard shadows.
- Keep corners short, borders fine, and repeated content in editorial rows or operational tables instead of nested cards.
- Use the existing self-hosted Public Sans family and standard semantic web controls.

## Public information

- The shared public navigation identifies the Kalibo MDRRMO SOU and links Home, About, How It Works, News & Advisories, reporting, language, theme, and administrator sign-in.
- The public footer leads with the three latest currently visible CMS posts. Draft, future-scheduled, archived, and expired items never appear there. When no public posts exist, show a factual empty state.
- Image attachments in public news articles render inline with alt text and a download link. Non-image files remain downloadable links.

## Operational surfaces

- Staff and admin pages share the palette and masthead treatment while prioritizing scanability, clear labels, and reachable actions.
- Keep severity, report status, and error states explicit in both text and color. Limit glass effects where they would reduce dense text or table contrast.

## Product and accessibility constraints

- Keep hotline guidance unmistakable and never imply that submitting a report dispatches responders.
- Preserve existing routes, role checks, form behavior, private tracking boundaries, language support, and low-bandwidth server rendering.
- Maintain keyboard focus visibility, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and truthful content. Never invent official contacts, advisories, or response guarantees.
